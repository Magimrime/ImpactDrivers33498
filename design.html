Autonomous Mode Evolution

V1: Simple Shooter

Strategy: Shoot 3 balls into the goal from a static position.

Challenge: Inconsistent accuracy. Slight misplacement of the robot would cause it to miss all shots, leading to wasted autonomous periods.

🔄 🛡️

V2: Dynamic Movement

Strategy: Prioritize movement and safety. The robot strafes or turns to leave the shooting zone.

Improvement: By moving to a parking zone or a better vantage point, we secured reliable points. We refined this further to "Turn & Move" instead of just strafing, which proved to be a safer maneuver for our specific chassis.
